## About

I develop statistical methods that turn large-scale genetic and genomic data into biological insights. I am especially interested in matrix factorization, graph regularization, multi-trait GWAS, and high-dimensional data analysis. I also care about using data and statistical tools to understand social and mental-health challenges.

## Selected work

- **[FLARE](https://fan-zhongyu.github.io/#research)** — Functionally informed, regularized sparse matrix factorization for multi-trait GWAS summary statistics. *In development.*
- **[ABM-SEIR Simulator](https://github.com/fan-zhongyu/ABM-SEIR-Simulation)** — A stochastic agent-based epidemic model built in R and Shiny for exploring how individual behavior shapes population-level dynamics.
- **[Ukraine Youth and Family Longitudinal Study](https://sites.brown.edu/globalflourishing/research/youth-war-displacement/uls/)** — Dyadic analysis of war-related psychological distress among Ukrainian adolescents and their caregivers.
- **[The Life of a Signal](https://fan-zhongyu.github.io/projects/the-life-of-a-signal/)** — An interactive nonlinear model of how signals spread, fade, and respond to intervention across a social network.
